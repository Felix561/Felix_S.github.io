A little about me

I am a fifth-semester M.Sc. Artificial Intelligence student at OTH Amberg-Weiden with a practical focus on deep learning, evaluation workflows, and benchmark-oriented work.

My current interests include reasoning systems, transformer-based approaches, and test-time computation methods, especially in the context of the ARC-AGI benchmark.

Alongside my studies, I worked as an AI engineer on image classification at TIKI GmbH, and also worked at deXcon GmbH. My broader hands-on work includes OCR, language processing, small RAG applications, and computer vision. My academic background started in engineering through a B.Eng. in Renewable Energy Engineering rather than classical computer science.
